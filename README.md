# 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes **warehouse performance** and **shipment distribution** in an FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) supply chain. The objective is to identify operational factors affecting shipment volume, evaluate warehouse utilization, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve inventory distribution and logistics efficiency.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow consisting of **Python for data cleaning**, **Excel for exploratory analysis**, and **Power BI for dashboard development**.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The company operates multiple warehouses across different regions; however, shipment volumes are unevenly distributed. While some warehouses handle significantly higher shipment volumes, others remain underutilized, making inventory allocation and operational planning less efficient.

The company needs analytical insights to identify the factors influencing warehouse performance and improve supply chain operations.

- 📦 Which warehouses contribute the highest shipment volume?

# 💡 Key Insights

### 📦 Warehouse Performance

- Shipment volume is concentrated in a small number of high-performing warehouses.
- Several warehouses remain significantly underutilized.

---

### 🌎 Regional Performance

- **North** and **West** regions contribute the largest shipment volumes.
- **East** records the lowest shipment performance.

---

### 🏢 Warehouse Capacity

- Warehouse capacity shows only a weak relationship with shipment volume.
- Larger warehouses do not necessarily process more shipments.

---

### 🚚 Transportation

- Warehouses experiencing transportation issues generally record lower shipment volumes.
- Logistics reliability appears more influential than warehouse size.

---

### ❄ Infrastructure

- Warehouses equipped with **temperature regulation machines** achieve higher average shipment volumes.
- Operational facilities contribute more to performance than geographical location.

---

# 📈 Business Recommendations

- ✅ Balance inventory allocation across warehouses to reduce operational imbalance.
- ✅ Improve transportation reliability to minimize shipment disruptions.
- ✅ Expand temperature-controlled facilities where operationally beneficial.
- ✅ Replicate best practices from top-performing warehouses.
- ✅ Focus investment on operational efficiency rather than warehouse expansion.
- ✅ Investigate operational challenges in the East region to improve utilization.
